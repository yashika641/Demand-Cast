A **hybrid Prophet + ML/DL model** is built by combining Prophet’s **trend/seasonality strengths** with ML/DL’s ability to capture **nonlinear residual patterns**.

Let’s break it step by step:

---

## 🔹 Step 1: Train Prophet Model

```python
from prophet import Prophet

# df must have columns 'ds' (date) and 'y' (sales)
prophet = Prophet()
prophet.fit(df)

future = prophet.make_future_dataframe(periods=30)
forecast = prophet.predict(future)

# Prophet’s predictions
df["prophet_yhat"] = forecast["yhat"].iloc[:len(df)].values
```

---

## 🔹 Step 2: Get Residuals from Prophet

Residual = **Actual Sales – Prophet Prediction**

```python
df["residual"] = df["y"] - df["prophet_yhat"]
```

This residual contains the **patterns Prophet missed** (e.g., sudden spikes due to promotions, nonlinear effects, volatility).

---

## 🔹 Step 3: Train ML/DL Model on Residuals

### Option A: **XGBoost (fast & reliable)**

* Create features (lag sales, rolling averages, day-of-week, month).
* Train XGBoost to predict the residual.

```python
import xgboost as xgb
from sklearn.model_selection import train_test_split

# Example feature engineering
df["lag1"] = df["y"].shift(1)
df["lag7"] = df["y"].shift(7)
df["dayofweek"] = df["ds"].dt.dayofweek
df["month"] = df["ds"].dt.month
df = df.dropna()

X = df[["lag1", "lag7", "dayofweek", "month"]]
y = df["residual"]

X_train, X_test, y_train, y_test = train_test_split(X, y, test_size=0.2, shuffle=False)

xgb_model = xgb.XGBRegressor()
xgb_model.fit(X_train, y_train)
```

### Option B: **LSTM (good for long-term sequential patterns)**

* Use sliding windows of past sales (and Prophet residuals).
* Train LSTM to predict residuals.

---

## 🔹 Step 4: Make Hybrid Forecast

Final Forecast = **Prophet Forecast + ML Residual Prediction**

```python
# Prophet base forecast
future_forecast = prophet.predict(future)
base_pred = future_forecast["yhat"]

# Predict residuals with XGBoost
future_X = df[["lag1", "lag7", "dayofweek", "month"]].iloc[-30:]  # example for 30 days ahead
residual_pred = xgb_model.predict(future_X)

# Hybrid prediction
hybrid_forecast = base_pred[-30:].values + residual_pred
```

---

## 🔹 Step 5: Evaluate

Compare Prophet vs. XGBoost vs. Hybrid using metrics:

* RMSE, MAPE, MAE.
  You’ll usually see:
* Prophet alone: good trend/seasonality.
* XGBoost alone: good short-term volatility but misses long trends.
* **Hybrid: best of both** ✅

---

## 🔹 Summary of Hybrid Workflow

1. Prophet → captures **trend + seasonality**.
2. Compute residuals.
3. Train ML/DL (XGBoost/LSTM) on **residuals** using lag/temporal features.
4. Hybrid forecast = Prophet forecast + ML/DL residual prediction.

---

## 🔹 Must-Have Components on a Stockout Dashboard

### 1. **KPI Cards (Top Metrics)**

Show high-level numbers at the top:

* **Stockout Rate (%)** → % of demand events that ended in stockout
* **Lost Sales (Qty & Value)** → how much demand was unfulfilled
* **Service Level (%)** → % of demand successfully fulfilled
* **Top 5 Stockout Products** → ranked by lost sales
* **Average Stockout Duration** (if tracked)

---

### 2. **Stockout Trend Over Time**

* Line chart of **stockout rate (%)** over days/weeks/months.
* Helps spot **seasonality or sudden spikes**.

---

### 3. **Product-Wise Stockout Analysis**

* Bar chart/table:

  * Products with **highest lost sales**
  * Products with **highest stockout frequency**
* Pareto analysis (80/20 → 20% of products cause 80% stockouts).

---

### 4. **Region / Store / Warehouse Impact**

* Heatmap or map view showing **geographic spread of stockouts**.
* Which regions/customers are most affected?

---

### 5. **Stockout by Category**

* Break down stockouts by **product category (e.g., Electronics, Grocery, Apparel)**.

---

### 6. **Lost Sales Value Over Time**

* Line chart of **lost revenue due to stockouts**.
* Option to filter by product, region, supplier.

---

### 7. **Root Cause Breakdown** *(if data available)*

* Pie chart or stacked bar:

  * Forecasting errors
  * Supplier delay
  * Low reorder point
  * Unexpected demand spike

---

### 8. **Inventory Health Indicators**

* % of SKUs **at risk of stockout** (low inventory, near reorder level).
* Average **lead time** vs. actual replenishment.

---

### 9. **Filters / Slicers**

Interactive filters for:

* Date range
* Product / Category
* Region / Store
* Supplier

---

### 10. **Alerts & Recommendations** (Optional but Powerful 🚀)

* List of **products at critical stock levels** (real-time alert).
* AI/ML forecast: *“Product X likely to stockout in 7 days”*.
* Suggested **safety stock adjustment**.

---

## 🔹 Dashboard Layout (Best Practice)

* **Top Section** → KPIs (cards)
* **Middle Section** → Trends (time-series charts)
* **Bottom Section** → Breakdown (products, regions, root causes)
* **Side Panel** → Filters

---
